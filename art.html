Meet the Artist

Juliane M. Liberto, Ph.D.

Scientist | Artist | Dog Mom

I am a cancer biologist currently living in Baltimore, where I spend my days exploring complex interactions between tumors and the immune system! My passion for science has always been driven by my curiosity and my desire to understand the world around me, but creativity has always been another way for me to explore and observe life.

I find inspiration in the small details: patterns found in nature, the beauty of biological systems, and the moments that make everyday life meaningful. This page is a collection of the things that inspire me beyond the laboratory.

Photography | My photography is inspired by the quiet details of the world around me. I enjoy capturing nature and architecture. I am particularly drawn to patterns, textures, and structures in all spaces - from tall skyscapers to our microscopic world. These images are a collection of moments where I paused to appreciate something unexpected, captured with nothing more than the camera I carry with me every day: my iPhone.

Ceramics | My claywork captures the beauty in the imperfections. I appreciate that no two pieces are ever exactly alike and I enjoy the balance between control and discovery that make handmade work unique.>

Poetry | My writting reflects different stages of my life, capturing moments of growth, reflection, uncertainty, and discovery. Poetry gives me a space to explore emotions and experiences that are difficult to express in any other medium.

When I'm not at the bench or making art, you'll usually find me exploring Baltimore with my two dogs, Pochi and Chiyo, or in the kitchen experimenting with different recipies!

Loving You

Loving You feels like joyous celestrations purifying an infinite expanse with illumination. Colliding every so often to spread particles of light miles into the darkness. The openeing of doors to worlds far beyond the possibilities of our existence.

Loving You feels like six foot eyes staring with wonder through bright lights and patterned rythms. Footsepts back and forth over familiar ground shaking with excitement and stolen glances amoungst a sea of welcoming bodies anticipating collective movements.

Loving You feels like sunflowers blooming on my skin. The flow of an old couuch. Time stopping for just moments at a time when smiles are shared across a room. Half eaten cookies on cold marble watching mangoes dance in our mouths.

Loving You feels like simple elegance under iconic monuments while waves perform an orchestra of music against our ears. Distant lights silhouette the water that gently curls theough our hair leaving salty spectacles on our lips.

To my forever, Loving You is loving the beautiful gfift of life.

-JML 01/07/2022

Stars

I saw the stars.

They didn't seem so distance today. Almost as if I were able to reach out and grasp one.

These are the same stars of stories once told. The stars that gave hope and guidance to those who would seek it.

As I gaze upon them, I am taken to worlds far beyond ours. Places and times not tainted by man, but that which hold an existent future for our species.

Man...we are nothing but a speck of dust in this vast universie. But we are made of these same stars, whose miraculous placement in this universe provide us the bility to sustain life...to think...to love...to be.

Is it not incredible?

The existence of such life and, even more, mans ability to understand our place in this infinate void.

But is it truley a void? Despite the never ending darkness of space that surrounds our little blue home, I have a spectacular faith of something beyond our comprehension.

A faith that is stronger than the winds that blow through the tall mountaintops and fiercer than the fires that devour valleys. A faith that solidifies in my mind, we are meant to be here.

We are meant to be here exactly as we are, not in a time before nor in a time yet to come, but here and now.

So when I gaze upon the stars, I see a thousand different worlds and a thousand different lifetimes that God has planned for us.

And the stars become so much more than their physical self.

-JML 12/19/2012
